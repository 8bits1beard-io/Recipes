# Iowa Baked Spaghetti

*A recipe by Joshua Walderbach*

Iowa-style baked spaghetti, church-potluck style: a pork-and-beef meat sauce with charred sweet corn and a touch of brown sugar, layered over spaghetti with a creamy cream-cheese-and-sour-cream middle and a melty mozzarella top.

**Servings:** 8 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Total time:** ~1 hour 15 minutes (plus rest)

---

## Ingredients

### Meat Sauce
- 1 lb (16 oz) ground beef (80/20)
- 1 lb (16 oz) ground pork
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced (~5 oz)
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 jar (24 oz) marinara sauce
- 3/4 cup (~4 oz) sweet corn kernels, drained (fire-roasted if you like)
- 1 tbsp (~0.5 oz) brown sugar
- 1 tbsp Italian seasoning
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

### Cream Layer
- 8 oz cream cheese, softened
- 1/2 cup (~4 oz) sour cream

### Pasta & Topping
- 1 lb (16 oz) spaghetti
- 2 cups (8 oz) shredded mozzarella

---

## Instructions

1. **Heat oven & boil the spaghetti.** Preheat the oven to 350°F. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the spaghetti until just shy of al dente — a minute or two less than the package says, since it finishes cooking in the oven. Drain and set aside.

2. **Brown the meat.** In a large skillet or pot over medium-high heat, cook the ground beef and ground pork together, breaking them into crumbles, until browned and cooked through. Spoon off excess grease if there's a lot, but leave a little for flavor.

3. **Cook the aromatics.** Add the onion to the meat and cook until soft, then add the garlic for about 30 seconds until fragrant.

4. **Build the sauce.** Stir in the marinara, corn, brown sugar, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper. Let it simmer gently to let the flavors meld — the sweet corn and brown sugar against the savory pork is the Iowa signature here. Taste and adjust.

5. **Make the cream layer.** In a small bowl, stir together the cream cheese and sour cream until smooth and spreadable. This is your creamy middle layer.

6. **Assemble the casserole.** Grease a 9x13 baking dish. Spread half the cooked spaghetti across the bottom, dollop and spread the cream cheese mixture over it, then spoon over half the meat sauce. Add the remaining spaghetti, then the rest of the sauce. Top evenly with the mozzarella.

7. **Bake.** Bake at 350°F until the casserole is bubbling at the edges and the cheese is melted and lightly golden, about 30 minutes.

8. **Rest, then serve.** Let it rest for 10 minutes before serving so the layers set and slice cleanly instead of sliding apart.

---

## Swaps & Tips

- Tie it to the Iowa Bachelor by using 1 lb Italian or spicy pork sausage in place of the ground pork.
- Cottage cheese can stand in for the cream cheese and sour cream layer — an even more old-school Midwest move.
- Fire-roasted corn deepens the Iowa note; a handful of shredded cheddar mixed into the mozzarella top leans more Midwest.
- Make-ahead friendly: assemble, refrigerate, and add about 10 minutes to the bake time.
- Jarred marinara is already salted, so taste before adding the full teaspoon of salt.
