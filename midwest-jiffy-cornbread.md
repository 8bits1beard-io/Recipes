# Midwest Jiffy Cornbread

*A recipe by Joshua Walderbach*

Doctored Jiffy cornbread baked up moist, rich, and lightly sweet — sour cream and butter for tenderness, a little honey for Midwest sweetness, and real sweet corn folded in. Best in a hot cast-iron skillet for crisp edges.

**Servings:** 9 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Total time:** ~45 minutes

---

## Ingredients

- 2 boxes (8.5 oz each) Jiffy Corn Muffin Mix
- 2 large eggs
- 1/2 cup (4 fl oz) milk
- 1/2 cup (~4 oz) sour cream
- 4 tbsp (2 oz) unsalted butter, melted (plus a little for the pan)
- 2 tbsp (~1.5 oz) honey
- 1/2 cup (~3 oz) sweet corn kernels, drained
- 1/2 cup (2 oz) shredded sharp cheddar (optional)

---

## Instructions

1. **Heat oven & prep the pan.** Preheat the oven to 375°F. If using a 10-inch cast-iron skillet, drop in a pat of butter and set the skillet in the oven to heat while you mix — a hot, buttered skillet is what gives you crisp golden edges. For an 8x8 or 9-inch square pan, just grease it well.

2. **Mix the wet ingredients.** In a large bowl, whisk together the eggs, milk, sour cream, melted butter, and honey until smooth.

3. **Add the mix.** Stir in the Jiffy mix just until combined — a few lumps are fine, and overmixing makes the cornbread tough. Fold in the corn and cheddar.

4. **Rest the batter.** Let the batter rest for about 4 minutes so the cornmeal hydrates and the leavening activates — this gives you a more tender, even crumb.

5. **Bake.** Pour the batter into the hot skillet (careful, it'll sizzle) or your prepared pan, and bake at 375°F for 25–30 minutes, until the top is golden and a toothpick in the center comes out clean.

6. **Cool & serve.** Let the cornbread cool for about 10 minutes before slicing so it sets up. Serve warm with butter and a drizzle of honey.

---

## Swaps & Tips

- For a single box, halve everything and bake in an 8x8 pan (or as ~8 muffins at 400°F for 15–18 min).
- Cast iron gives the crispiest, most caramelized edges — a greased 8- or 9-inch square pan works fine too.
- **Spicy twist (optional):** Fold in 1–2 finely diced jalapeños (seed them for milder heat, leave the seeds for more) and swap the cheddar for pepper jack. For an extra kick, whisk 1/4 tsp cayenne or a few dashes of hot sauce into the wet ingredients. Keep the honey here — the sweet-heat contrast against the jalapeño is the best part. Pickled jalapeños work too and add a little tang.
- Finish with honey butter (equal parts softened butter and honey, whipped) while still warm.
- Pairs perfectly with the Cedar Rapids Meatloaf, or with chili.

---

### A note on the milk and temperature

The milk is dropped to 1/2 cup (from the 2/3 cup two boxes would normally call for) on purpose — the sour cream and melted butter add their own moisture, so the full amount would make the batter too wet. And it bakes at 375°F instead of the 400°F on the box, since the richer, sweeter batter browns faster.
