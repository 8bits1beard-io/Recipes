# Midwest Jiffy Cornbread

*A recipe by Joshua Walderbach*

Doctored Jiffy cornbread baked up moist, rich, and lightly sweet — sour cream and butter for tenderness, a little honey for Midwest sweetness, and real sweet corn folded in. Best in a hot cast-iron skillet for crisp edges.

**Yield:** 9 servings &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Active time:** ~10 minutes &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Total time:** ~45 minutes

---

## Ingredients

- 2 boxes (8.5 oz / 241 g each) Jiffy Corn Muffin Mix
- 2 large eggs
- 1/2 cup (120 mL) milk
- 1/2 cup (~4 oz / 120 g) sour cream
- 4 tbsp (2 oz / 57 g) unsalted butter, melted, plus a little for the pan
- 2 tbsp (~1.5 oz / 43 g) honey
- 1/2 cup (~3 oz / 85 g) sweet corn kernels, drained
- 1/2 cup (2 oz / 57 g) shredded sharp cheddar, optional

---

## Equipment

- 10-inch cast-iron skillet (or an 8x8-inch or 9-inch square pan)

---

## Instructions

1. **Heat oven and prep the pan.** Preheat the oven to **375°F (190°C)**. If using a cast-iron skillet, drop in a pat of butter and set the skillet in the oven to heat while you mix — a hot, buttered skillet is what gives you crisp golden edges. For an 8x8-inch or 9-inch square pan, just grease it well.

2. **Mix the wet ingredients.** In a large bowl, whisk together the eggs, milk, sour cream, melted butter, and honey until smooth.

3. **Add the mix.** Stir in the Jiffy mix just until combined — a few lumps are fine, and overmixing makes the cornbread tough. Fold in the corn and cheddar.

4. **Rest the batter.** Let the batter rest for about **4 minutes** so the cornmeal hydrates and the leavening activates — this gives you a more tender, even crumb.

5. **Bake.** Pour the batter into the hot skillet (careful, it'll sizzle) or your prepared pan, and bake at **375°F (190°C)** for **25–30 minutes**, until the top is golden and a toothpick in the center comes out clean.

6. **Cool and serve.** Let the cornbread cool for about **10 minutes** before slicing so it sets up. Serve warm with butter and a drizzle of honey.

---

## Notes

- The milk is dropped to **1/2 cup** (from the **2/3 cup** two boxes would normally call for) on purpose — the sour cream and melted butter add their own moisture, so the full amount would make the batter too wet.
- It bakes at **375°F (190°C)** instead of the **400°F (205°C)** on the box, since the richer, sweeter batter browns faster.

---

## Swaps & Tips

- For a single box, halve everything and bake in an 8x8-inch pan, or as ~8 muffins at **400°F (205°C)** for **15–18 minutes**.
- Cast iron gives the crispiest, most caramelized edges — a greased 8- or 9-inch square pan works fine too.
- **Spicy twist:** Fold in **1–2** finely diced jalapeños (seed them for milder heat, leave the seeds for more) and swap the cheddar for pepper jack. For an extra kick, whisk **1/4 tsp** cayenne or a few dashes of hot sauce into the wet ingredients. Keep the honey here — the sweet-heat contrast against the jalapeño is the best part. Pickled jalapeños work too and add a little tang.
- Finish with honey butter (equal parts softened butter and honey, whipped) while still warm.
- Pairs perfectly with the [Cedar Rapids Meatloaf](./cedar-rapids-meatloaf.md), or with chili.

---

## Storage

Keep covered at room temperature for up to 2 days, or refrigerate for up to 5 days. Reheat slices in a warm oven or a buttered skillet to bring the edges back.
