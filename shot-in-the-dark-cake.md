# Shot in the Dark Cake

*A recipe by Joshua Walderbach*

A moist 9x13-inch chocolate sheet cake with buttermilk, hot coffee, and a cocoa-blooming step for deeper chocolate flavor. The batter comes out thin on purpose — that's what keeps the crumb tender.

**Yield:** one 9x13-inch cake (~15 servings) &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Active time:** ~20 minutes &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Total time:** ~2 1/2 hours (including cooling)

---

## Ingredients

### Cake
- 220 g all-purpose flour
- 400 g granulated sugar
- 65 g natural unsweetened cocoa powder
- 4 g (1 tsp) baking powder
- 9 g (2 tsp) baking soda
- 4–5 g (1 tsp) kosher salt
- 2 large eggs (about 100 g without shells)
- 240 mL (1 cup) cultured buttermilk
- 120 mL (1/2 cup) canola oil
- 5 mL (1 tsp) pure vanilla extract
- 240 mL (1 cup) hot brewed coffee

### Frosting
- 454 g (16 oz) prepared dark chocolate fudge frosting

### Optional Topping
- 30–55 g chopped dark and/or milk chocolate chips

---

## Equipment

- 9x13-inch baking pan
- Baking sheet, if using a disposable aluminum pan
- Mixer with whisk or paddle attachment
- Heat-safe bowl or measuring jug for blooming the cocoa
- Spatula
- Fork, toothpick, or thin knife for doneness testing

---

## Instructions

1. **Preheat the oven.** Set the oven to **350°F (175°C)** using regular Bake, with the rack in the center.

2. **Prepare the pan.** Grease the 9x13-inch pan. If using a disposable aluminum pan, place it on a sturdy baking sheet before filling it.

3. **Brew the coffee.** Brew **240 mL hot coffee** and keep it hot.

4. **Bloom the cocoa.** Put the **65 g cocoa powder** in a heat-safe bowl or measuring jug. Slowly whisk in the **240 mL hot coffee** until smooth. Let it stand for **2–3 minutes**.

   > Blooming the cocoa in hot liquid dissolves flavor compounds and intensifies the chocolate character.

5. **Combine the remaining dry ingredients.** In the mixer bowl, combine:

   - 220 g all-purpose flour
   - 400 g granulated sugar
   - 4 g baking powder
   - 9 g baking soda
   - 4–5 g kosher salt

   Mix on very low speed for about **20–30 seconds** to distribute the leaveners and salt evenly.

6. **Add the wet ingredients.** Add:

   - 2 eggs
   - 240 mL buttermilk
   - 120 mL canola oil
   - 5 mL vanilla extract

   Mix on low speed for about **30 seconds**.

7. **Scrape and mix.** Scrape the sides and bottom of the bowl, then mix on **medium speed** for about **1 minute**.

8. **Add the bloomed cocoa mixture.** Turn the mixer to low and slowly pour in the cocoa-coffee mixture. Mix for about **30 seconds**, just until evenly combined. Scrape the bowl once more if needed.

   The batter will be quite thin. **Do not add extra flour.**

9. **Fill the pan.** Pour the batter into the prepared pan and level it gently.

10. **Bake.** Bake at **350°F (175°C)** for approximately **35–40 minutes**, checking from around **32 minutes**.

    The cake is done when a tester inserted into the center comes out with no wet batter. A few moist crumbs are fine.

11. **Cool completely.** Leave the cake uncovered in the pan until completely cool, usually about **1 1/2–2 hours**. Do not cover or frost while warm.

12. **Frost.** Once the cake is completely cool, spread the **454 g (16 oz)** tub of dark chocolate fudge frosting evenly over the top.

13. **Optional finish.** Sprinkle **30–55 g** chopped dark and/or milk chocolate chips over the frosting.

---

## Notes

- Use **all-purpose flour**, not bread flour, for a more tender crumb.
- Canola oil keeps the cake soft and moist even after cooling.
- Buttermilk contributes acidity, tenderness, and flavor while also reacting with the baking soda.
- Coffee deepens the perceived chocolate flavor without making the cake taste strongly of coffee.
- Keep the **vanilla extract** in the recipe even though it seems minor — it rounds out the chocolate flavor.
- For best consistency, weigh dry ingredients in grams and measure liquids in milliliters.

---

## Swaps & Tips

- Decaf coffee, or even hot water, works if you'd rather avoid the caffeine — you'll lose a little depth but the cake still bakes the same.
- Homemade buttermilk substitute: 240 mL milk plus **1 tbsp (15 mL)** lemon juice or white vinegar, rested **5 minutes**.
- Frost from the center outward with an offset spatula; a fully cooled cake won't tear.

---

## Storage

Cover the completely cooled and frosted cake and store at room temperature for short-term serving. For longer storage, refrigerate and let slices come closer to room temperature before serving for the best texture.
